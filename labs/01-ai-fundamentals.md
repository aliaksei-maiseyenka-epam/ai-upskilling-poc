# Lab 1: AI Fundamentals

## Core AI Concepts to Learn

### 1. AI Agents
- **What**: Programs that can perceive their environment and take actions
- **Example**: A chatbot that can answer questions and perform tasks

### 2. Prompt Engineering
- **What**: The art of writing effective instructions for AI models
- **Example**: "You are a helpful assistant. Answer in a friendly tone."

### 3. RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)
- **What**: AI that can search through documents to answer questions
- **Example**: AI that reads your company docs and answers questions about them

### 4. Embeddings
- **What**: Converting text into numbers that AI can understand and compare
- **Example**: Finding similar documents or questions
